Traditional Indian Medicine

The Millennia-Old Healing Systems of Ayurveda and Siddha

Roots and Philosophy

Indian medicine belongs to the oldest documented medical systems of humankind. At its center stands Ayurveda (the "knowledge of life"), whose origins date back over 3,000 years. Parallel to this, theSiddha system exists in southern India, which is strongly influenced by alchemy. Both systems view health not as the mere absence of disease, but as a dynamic balance of body, mind, and environment.

Interesting and Historical Facts

Sushruta: The Forefather of Surgery

As early as 600 BCE, the Indian physician Sushruta described highly complex surgical procedures in his compendium. He is considered a pioneer of plastic surgery, as he developed methods for nose reconstruction (rhinoplasty) that are still used in their basic principles today.

Focus on Phytotherapy

Indian medicine utilizes thousands of medicinal plants. Modern laboratories research these intensively. A well-known example is Ashwagandha (Indian ginseng/poison gooseberry), whose stress-reducing effects (adaptogen) are studied in clinical trials, or the anti-inflammatory effects of Turmeric (curcumin).

Recognized Medical System

In India, Ayurveda is legally on equal footing with Western medicine (allopathy). There are state universities, hospitals, and a dedicated ministry (AYUSH) that regulates the education and quality assurance of these traditional healing practices.
