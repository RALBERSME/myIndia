Tips for a Safe Journey

  • Health: Drink only filtered or bottled water ("Cook it, peel it, boil it, or forget it"). Check your vaccinations well in advance.
  • Culture & Clothing: RRespect local customs. Cover shoulders and knees and remove shoes when entering temples.
  • Transport: For longer distances, use registered taxis or apps like Uber/Ola. For a true adventure, take a tuk-tuk .. or simply click to the next website!
