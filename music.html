The Music of India

A spiritual journey from millennia-old classical ragas to the high-energy sound of modern Bollywood.

Traditional Instruments

Indian music is characterized by unique instruments that often generate overtones not commonly found in Western instruments. The most well-known are:

🪕

Sitar

Arguably the most famous Indian stringed instrument. In addition to the melody strings, it features numerous sympathetic resonance strings that produce the typically floating, Indian sound.

🥁

Tabla

A pair of small drums played with the fingers and palms. They allow for extremely complex and fast rhythmic patterns (talas).

🪵

Bansuri

A classical side-blown flute made of bamboo. It has a deep, warm, and highly emotional sound and is closely associated with Lord Krishna.

🎹

Harmonium

Although originally brought over by Europeans, this small keyboard instrument with bellows is now the centerpiece of almost all Indian vocal accompaniment.

Where do the melodies come from? (The Raga System)

In Indian music, there are no classical chords like in the West. The melodies are based on what are known as ragas. A raga is far more than a musical scale: it is a musical framework that evokes a very specific mood or atmosphere (rasa).

🎭 The connection to time and nature: TTraditionally, each raga is tied to a specific time of day (e.g., morning or midnight ragas) or even to a season (such as the monsoon). It is believed that a raga only unfolds its full spiritual effect when it is performed at the right time.

What is currently most popular?

The Indian musical landscape is dominated by two massive movements:

  • Bollywood-Musik (Filmi): By far the most popular musical style. Nearly every Indian movie is a musical. The soundtracks blend Indian classical elements with Western pop, hip-hop, and electronic beats. They are played everywhere in India—on the radio, in clubs, and in autorickshaws.
  • Der Boom von I-Pop & Desi-Hip-Hop: Independent music (Independent Pop) outside of cinema is growing rapidly. Punjabi Pop and Indian rap are exceptionally popular, fusing traditional rhythms (Bhangra beats) with modern club sounds. // Famous Singers & Musicians

Well-known singers & musicians

Ravi Shankar

Classical Sitar Legend

He made Indian music famous in the West, performed at the Woodstock Festival, and inspired the Beatles (particularly George Harrison).

A. R. Rahman

Film Composer & "Mozart of Madras"

The Academy Award-winning composer (known for Slumdog Millionaire, among others) revolutionized Bollywood music through the brilliant use of synthesizers and Western orchestras.

Arijit Singh

The King of Modern Ballads

Arguably the most popular playback singer in India today. His melancholic and romantic love songs in Bollywood movies have garnered billions of clicks on streaming platforms.

Diljit Dosanjh

Punjabi-Pop & Global Superstar

He brought high-energy Punjabi music to the world's biggest stages (e.g., as the first Indian artist to perform at the Coachella Festival) and fills stadiums worldwide.
